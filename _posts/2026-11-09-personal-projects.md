Hello I am avoiding chores again!

I've done a lot of things in my career, and now that I am living that consultant life, I am doing even *more* things. Something I have struggled a lot with is how to keep all those skills sharp! There's so little time left in the day after you consider 8+ hours of jorb and then chores and then *trying* to have a social life... Some days I have more spare energy than others. However, all it takes is one spell of low-energy and then any personal projects go to die. I can do sprints, but marathons are a lot more challenging.

With my experiences pair programming, I am wondering if there is a way to expand this practice into personal projects! I am a lot more motivated when I am accountable to another human being.

The other question is what projects I would like to even make. There are many areas I would like to improve upon:
- DBA skillset to handle more complex queries
- Spinning up a kubernetes environment
- Hosting some kind of personal python/react application for timesheets
- Aquarium health documentation app?
- Open source image hosting platform
- Personal "stack overflow" server for projects/learnings

I don't think any of these are going to happen before the year-end. But! I do think my new years resolution will be to figure out some kind of personal project to carry forward on my own. 

Before the year's up, I have some kubernetes trainings I paid for and ultimately never finished (because of that aformentioned low-energy thing). I think I want to try doing that and getting my certification so that I can spin up my own personal kubernetes ecosystem. To do what with? I dunno... that is next year's problem!
